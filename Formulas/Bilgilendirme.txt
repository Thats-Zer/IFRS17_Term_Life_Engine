IFRS17 TERM LIFE ENGINE
FORMUL VE MANTIK DOKUMANI

Baslangic Notu

Bu dokuman, mevcut term-life engine icindeki basitlestirilmis aktueryal ve
IFRS 17 olcum mantigini aciklar. Asagidaki formuller guncel kod ile uyumludur.

Model uretim kalibrasyonuna sahip degildir. Egitim, test ve portfoy gosterimi
amaciyla tasarlanmistir.


===========================================================
1) GENEL MODEL YAPISI
===========================================================

Tanimlar:

i  = police indeksi
t  = projeksiyon yili
n  = police sayisi
T  = projeksiyon ufku

Engine her policeyi zaman boyunca ayri ayri projekte eder:

Projection(i, t)

Her police-yil satiri icin hesaplanan ana alanlar:

> yas ve in-force durumu
> mortalite ve lapse varsayimlari
> survival ratio
> prim, hasar, gider, surrender ve reasurans nakit akislari
> bugunku deger nakit akislari
> BEL
> Risk Adjustment
> CSM
> IFRS 17 gruplama
> senaryo sonuclari


===========================================================
2) MORTALITE, LAPSE VE SURVIVAL
===========================================================

Mortalite:

qx(t)
=
current_age'e gore mortalite tablosundan okunan oran

Tablo kullanilmiyorsa:

qx(t)
=
base_mort_rate + age_factor * (current_age - min_issue_age)

Senaryo mortalite soku:

qx_scenario(t)
=
clip(qx(t) * mortality_shock_multiplier + mortality_shock, 0, 1)

Lapse:

lapse_rate(t)
=
lapse_base_rate * exp(-lapse_decay * (t - 1))

Survival multiplier:

survival_multiplier(t)
=
(1 - qx(t)) * (1 - lapse_rate(t))

Yil basi survival ratio:

survival_ratio(t)
=
1'den t-1'e kadar survival multiplier carpimi

Bu nedenle Year 1 icin survival_ratio = 1.


===========================================================
3) PRIM VE NAKIT AKISI MANTIGI
===========================================================

Brut prim girisi:

Gross_Premium_Inflow(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * gross_annual_premium

Olum tazminati:

Death_Benefits(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * adjusted_qx(t) * sum_assured

Surrender benefit:

Surrender_Benefit(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * lapse_rate(t)
* max(net_annual_premium * surrender_value_rate - surrender_charge(t), 0)

Operasyonel gider:

Operating_Expenses(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * (1 - lapse_rate(t)) * total_expense(t)

Reasurans ceding:

Reinsurance_Ceding(t)
=
Gross_Premium_Inflow(t) * reinsurance_cost_rate

Reasurans recovery:

Reinsurance_Recovery(t)
=
Death_Benefits(t) * reinsurance_cost_rate

Karsi taraf default maliyeti:

Counterparty_Default_Cost(t)
=
Reinsurance_Recovery(t) * counterparty_pd * counterparty_lgd

Net nakit akisi:

Net_Cash_Flow(t)
=
Total_Inflows(t) - Total_Outflows(t)


===========================================================
4) ISKONTO VE ZAMANLAMA KONVANSIYONU
===========================================================

Donem sonu iskonto faktoru:

discount_factor(t)
=
1 / (1 + r)^t

Donem basi iskonto faktoru:

discount_factor_opening(t)
=
1 / (1 + r)^(t - 1)

Engine zamanlama konvansiyonu:

> Prim girisleri: donem basi iskonto faktoru
> Reasurans ceding primleri: donem basi iskonto faktoru
> Hasarlar: donem sonu iskonto faktoru
> Giderler: donem sonu iskonto faktoru
> Surrender benefit: donem sonu iskonto faktoru
> Reasurans recoveries: donem sonu iskonto faktoru
> Karsi taraf default maliyeti: donem sonu iskonto faktoru

Toplam PV inflow:

PV_Total_Inflows
=
PV_Gross_Premium_Inflow + PV_Reinsurance_Recovery

Toplam PV outflow:

PV_Total_Outflows
=
PV_Net_Death_Benefit
+ PV_Surrender_Benefit
+ PV_Operating_Expenses
+ PV_Reinsurance_Ceding
+ PV_Counterparty_Default_Cost

PV net cashflow:

PV_Net_Cash_Flow
=
PV_Total_Inflows - PV_Total_Outflows


===========================================================
5) BEST ESTIMATE LIABILITY (BEL)
===========================================================

Engine liability-positive BEL konvansiyonu kullanir:

BEL
=
PV(Outflows) - PV(Inflows)

Esdeger form:

BEL
=
-PV(Net_Cash_Flow)

Yorum:

> BEL > 0 net yukumluluk anlamina gelir.
> BEL < 0 net varlik pozisyonu anlamina gelir.

Cashflow ve BEL ayni ayarlanmis mortalite basis'ini kullanir:

Adjusted_qx(t)
=
qx(t) * (1 - lapse_mortality_correlation * lapse_rate(t))

Adjusted_Death_Benefits(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * adjusted_qx(t) * sum_assured

BEL liability cashflow:

PV_Adjusted_Liability_Cash_Flow(t)
=
PV(claims + surrender + expenses + reinsurance ceding + default cost)
- PV(premiums + reinsurance recovery)

Police BEL:

BEL(i)
=
t yillari boyunca PV_Adjusted_Liability_Cash_Flow(i, t) toplami


===========================================================
6) RISK ADJUSTMENT (RA)
===========================================================

Risk Adjustment, finansal olmayan risk belirsizligi icin talep edilen marji
temsil eder.

Mevcut engine basitlestirilmis stochastic confidence-level yaklasimi kullanir:

RA(i)
=
CoC_Ratio * max(Quantile_alpha(PV_Liability(i)) - Mean(PV_Liability(i)), 0)

Simule edilen liability dagilimi BEL ile ayni liability-positive zamanlama
konvansiyonunu kullanir.

Basitlestirilmis risk suruculeri:

> mortalite volatilitesi
> lapse volatilitesi
> gider volatilitesi
> karsi taraf default olaylari
> reasurans ceding ve recovery etkileri

Config parametreleri:

> confidence_level
> n_risk_scenarios
> coc_ratio
> ra_mortality_sigma
> ra_lapse_sigma
> ra_expense_sigma

Risk decomposition fonksiyonu yalnizca toplam RA dondurur. Sabit yuzdeli
dagilimlar gercek bir risk attribution kosusu olmadan yaniltici olacagi icin
kullanilmaz.


===========================================================
7) CONTRACTUAL SERVICE MARGIN (CSM)
===========================================================

CSM kazanilmamis gelecekteki kari temsil eder.

Onemli konvansiyon:

BEL zaten prim girislerini icerir. Bu nedenle CSM formulu icinde primler ikinci
kez dusulmez.

CSM oncesi toplam yukumluluk:

Total_Liability
=
BEL + RA

Baslangic CSM:

Initial_CSM
=
max(-(BEL + RA), 0)

Onerous loss:

Onerous_Loss
=
max(BEL + RA, 0)

Yorum:

> BEL + RA < 0 ise sozlesme/grup karlidir ve CSM pozitiftir.
> BEL + RA > 0 ise sozlesme/grup onerous kabul edilir ve CSM sifirdir.

PV premiums ciktilarda mutabakat bilgisi olarak tutulur, fakat CSM formulunde
ek bir indirim kalemi olarak kullanilmaz.


===========================================================
8) CSM RELEASE VE ROLLFORWARD
===========================================================

Coverage unit:

Coverage_Unit(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * (1 - lapse_rate(t))

Toplam coverage unit:

Total_Coverage
=
projeksiyon yillari boyunca Coverage_Unit(t) toplami

CSM release:

CSM_Release(t)
=
Opening_CSM * Coverage_Unit(t) / Total_Coverage

Engine tek reporting-period rollforward uretir. Bu nedenle csm_results.csv
icindeki release, config icindeki reporting_year icin hesaplanir.

Finance cost / accretion:

Finance_Cost
=
Opening_CSM * accretion_rate

Varsayilan olarak accretion_rate config icindeki discount_rate degeridir.
Prior locked rate verilirse o oran kullanilir.

Kapanis CSM:

CSM_Closing_Raw
=
CSM_Opening + Finance_Cost - CSM_Release - Unlocking_Adjustment

Raporlanan CSM:

CSM_Closing
=
max(CSM_Closing_Raw, 0)
