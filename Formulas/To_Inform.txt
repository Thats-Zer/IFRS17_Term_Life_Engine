IFRS17 TERM LIFE ENGINE
FORMULA & LOGIC PAPER

Starting Note

This document explains the simplified actuarial and IFRS 17 measurement logic
used by this educational term-life engine. The formulas below are aligned with
the current codebase.

The model is not production-calibrated. It is intended for learning, testing,
and portfolio demonstration.


===========================================================
1) GENERAL MODEL STRUCTURE
===========================================================

Let:

i  = policy index
t  = projection year
n  = number of policies
T  = projection horizon

The engine projects each policy separately through time:

Projection(i, t)

For every policy-year row, the engine calculates:

> age and in-force status
> mortality and lapse assumptions
> survival ratio
> premiums, claims, expenses, surrender and reinsurance cashflows
> present value cashflows
> BEL
> Risk Adjustment
> CSM
> IFRS 17 grouping
> scenario results


===========================================================
2) MORTALITY, LAPSE AND SURVIVAL
===========================================================

Mortality:

qx(t)
=
mortality table rate by current age

or, if no table is used:

qx(t)
=
base_mort_rate + age_factor * (current_age - min_issue_age)

Scenario mortality shock:

qx_scenario(t)
=
clip(qx(t) * mortality_shock_multiplier + mortality_shock, 0, 1)

Lapse:

lapse_rate(t)
=
lapse_base_rate * exp(-lapse_decay * (t - 1))

Survival multiplier:

survival_multiplier(t)
=
(1 - qx(t)) * (1 - lapse_rate(t))

Opening survival ratio:

survival_ratio(t)
=
product of survival multipliers from years 1 to t-1

Therefore, Year 1 has survival_ratio = 1.


===========================================================
3) PREMIUM AND CASHFLOW LOGIC
===========================================================

Gross premium inflow:

Gross_Premium_Inflow(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * gross_annual_premium

Death benefits:

Death_Benefits(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * qx(t) * sum_assured

Surrender benefits:

Surrender_Benefit(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * lapse_rate(t)
* max(net_annual_premium - surrender_charge(t), 0)

Operating expenses:

Operating_Expenses(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * (1 - lapse_rate(t)) * total_expense(t)

Reinsurance ceding:

Reinsurance_Ceding(t)
=
Gross_Premium_Inflow(t) * reinsurance_cost_rate

Reinsurance recovery:

Reinsurance_Recovery(t)
=
Death_Benefits(t) * reinsurance_cost_rate

Counterparty default cost:

Counterparty_Default_Cost(t)
=
Reinsurance_Recovery(t) * counterparty_pd * counterparty_lgd

Net cashflow:

Net_Cash_Flow(t)
=
Total_Inflows(t) - Total_Outflows(t)


===========================================================
4) DISCOUNTING AND TIMING CONVENTION
===========================================================

Closing present value factor:

discount_factor(t)
=
1 / (1 + r)^t

Opening present value factor:

discount_factor_opening(t)
=
1 / (1 + r)^(t - 1)

The engine uses this timing convention:

> Premium inflows: opening discount factor
> Reinsurance ceding premiums: opening discount factor
> Claims: closing discount factor
> Expenses: closing discount factor
> Surrender benefits: closing discount factor
> Reinsurance recoveries: closing discount factor
> Counterparty default costs: closing discount factor

Total PV inflows:

PV_Total_Inflows
=
PV_Gross_Premium_Inflow + PV_Reinsurance_Recovery

Total PV outflows:

PV_Total_Outflows
=
PV_Net_Death_Benefit
+ PV_Surrender_Benefit
+ PV_Operating_Expenses
+ PV_Reinsurance_Ceding
+ PV_Counterparty_Default_Cost

PV net cashflow:

PV_Net_Cash_Flow
=
PV_Total_Inflows - PV_Total_Outflows


===========================================================
5) BEST ESTIMATE LIABILITY (BEL)
===========================================================

The engine uses a liability-positive BEL convention:

BEL
=
PV(Outflows) - PV(Inflows)

Equivalent form:

BEL
=
-PV(Net_Cash_Flow)

Interpretation:

> BEL > 0 means a net liability.
> BEL < 0 means a net asset position.

BEL includes adjusted mortality and surrender assumptions:

Adjusted_qx(t)
=
qx(t) * (1 - lapse_mortality_correlation * lapse_rate(t))

Adjusted_Death_Benefits(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * adjusted_qx(t) * sum_assured

BEL liability cashflow:

PV_Adjusted_Liability_Cash_Flow(t)
=
PV(claims + surrender + expenses + reinsurance ceding + default cost)
- PV(premiums + reinsurance recovery)

Policy BEL:

BEL(i)
=
sum over t of PV_Adjusted_Liability_Cash_Flow(i, t)


===========================================================
6) RISK ADJUSTMENT (RA)
===========================================================

Risk Adjustment compensates for non-financial risk uncertainty.

The current engine uses a simplified stochastic confidence-level approach:

RA(i)
=
CoC_Ratio * max(Quantile_alpha(PV_Liability(i)) - Mean(PV_Liability(i)), 0)

The simulated liability distribution uses the same liability-positive timing
convention as BEL.

The simplified risk drivers are:

> mortality volatility
> lapse volatility
> expense volatility
> counterparty default events
> reinsurance ceding and recovery effects

Configuration parameters:

> confidence_level
> n_risk_scenarios
> coc_ratio
> ra_mortality_sigma
> ra_lapse_sigma
> ra_expense_sigma

The risk decomposition function returns total RA only. Fixed percentage splits
are intentionally avoided because they would be misleading without a true
component attribution run.


===========================================================
7) CONTRACTUAL SERVICE MARGIN (CSM)
===========================================================

CSM represents unearned future profit.

Important convention:

BEL already includes premium inflows. Therefore, premiums must not be
subtracted again in the CSM formula.

Total liability before CSM:

Total_Liability
=
BEL + RA

Initial CSM:

Initial_CSM
=
max(-(BEL + RA), 0)

Onerous loss:

Onerous_Loss
=
max(BEL + RA, 0)

Interpretation:

> If BEL + RA < 0, the contract/group is profitable and CSM is positive.
> If BEL + RA > 0, the contract/group is onerous and CSM is zero.

PV premiums are still stored as disclosure/reconciliation information, but they
are not used as an additional subtraction in the CSM formula.


===========================================================
8) CSM RELEASE AND ROLLFORWARD
===========================================================

Coverage units:

Coverage_Unit(t)
=
in_force(t) * survival_ratio(t) * (1 - lapse_rate(t))

Total coverage units:

Total_Coverage
=
sum over projection years of Coverage_Unit(t)

CSM release:

CSM_Release(t)
=
Opening_CSM * Coverage_Unit(t) / Total_Coverage

The engine produces a single reporting-period rollforward. Therefore it uses
the configured reporting_year for the release included in csm_results.csv.

Finance cost / accretion:

Finance_Cost
=
Opening_CSM * accretion_rate

By default, accretion_rate is the configured discount_rate unless a prior
locked rate is supplied.

Closing CSM:

CSM_Closing_Raw
=
CSM_Opening + Finance_Cost - CSM_Release - Unlocking_Adjustment

Reported CSM:

CSM_Closing
=
max(CSM_Closing_Raw, 0)


===========================================================
9) IFRS 17 GROUPING
===========================================================

The engine groups contracts using:

> business nature
> risk level
> lapse risk
> onerous status

Grouping is performed at policy level, not policy-year level.

Therefore:

Group policy count
=
number of unique policy_id values in the group

Group total sum assured
=
sum of each policy's sum_assured once

The group output is written to:

outputs/group_results.csv


===========================================================
10) SCENARIO ANALYSIS
===========================================================

Scenario runs rebuild the projection, cashflows, BEL, RA and CSM under shocked
assumptions.

Current scenario outputs include:

> scenario
> n_policies
> Total_BEL
> Total_RA
> Total_CSM_Opening
> Total_CSM_Closing
> Onerous_Count
> Onerous_Loss

Typical shocks:

> mortality_up_10
> lapse_up_20
> expense_up_15
> discount_down_100bps

12) FINAL NOTE
===========================================================

This document describes the current simplified engine behavior. Real IFRS 17
production models require actuarial calibration, governance, audit controls,

methodology approval.
